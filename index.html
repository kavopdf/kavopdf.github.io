Security & output

Prove who sent it, control who can open it, and remove what must not travel.

Cryptographically sign a PDF so anyone who opens it can confirm it came from you, and that it hasn't changed since.

Is installing an APK safe?

An APK is just Android's app file — the same format the Play Store installs for you. The only difference is that you're the one doing the installing, so Android asks for your permission first. KavoPDF asks for exactly one permission — the camera — and only while a scanner screen is open.

Permissions, and why

  • Camera — the only permission the app asks for, and only while the document scanner or the QR reader is open. Never in the background.
  • Files — no permission at all. You pick a document through Android's own file picker, so the app never asks for storage access and only ever sees the one file you chose.
  • No internet permission needed for editing — every tool runs on-device.

If a download ever looks like it came from somewhere other than this page, don't install it. The only official source is this site and the GitHub repository it links to.

The short version

KavoPDF processes your PDFs on your device. It does not upload your files, does not require an account, and does not sell or share your data. Used offline, nothing about your documents ever touches a network.

KavoPDF is built so that your documents stay yours. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you, by default. The app does not ask for your name, email or phone number, and it does not create a user profile. There is no sign-in.

Your files

PDFs you open, edit, merge, split, compress, sign, redact, scan or create are handled entirely on your phone. They are never copied to a server we control, and we have no way to see them.

Permissions

  • Camera — the only permission the app requests. Used only when you start the document scanner or the QR scanner, and only while that screen is open.
  • Files — no storage permission is requested. You choose a document through Android's own file picker, so the app sees only that file and nothing else on your device.

The app does not request access to your contacts, call logs, location or messages.

Network access

Core reading, editing, scanning and creation features work offline. This website contacts GitHub's public API to show you the newest release details; that request contains no information about you or your documents. If a future app version includes an online feature, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and it will be opt-in.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. The app does not embed advertising or analytics trackers that profile you across other apps.

Children

KavoPDF is a general-purpose document tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above will change too, and material changes will be noted on this page.
